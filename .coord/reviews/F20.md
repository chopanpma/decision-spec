## Design rulings confirmed

- `render_index` is deliberately NOT in `gen`'s artifact set and NOT in
  `is_managed_path` — otherwise gen's stale-artifact sweep would delete the
  index on every run. On-demand only. Correct call.
- Per-model diagrams are produced by shimming a one-model `Workspace` through
  the existing `mermaid_model` — no new renderer, F19 escaping still applies.
- Scenario rows flatten given/when/then (scenarios carry no EARS string —
  that text lives on the requirement). Documented limitation, acceptable for
  an index view.
